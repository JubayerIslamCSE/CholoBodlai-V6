CholoBodlai -- teacher demonstration script

1. Homepage: Open http://localhost:8080/ before login. Explain public access and two audience sections.
2. Register: Create a truck owner with one truck and unique email. Show that login waits for both email verification and admin approval.
3. Admin: Open /admin/mail-preview with APP_MAIL_PREVIEW=true in an admin browser. Open the verification link. In /admin approve the owner and vehicle.
4. Shipper: Request transport from Dhaka to Narayanganj with a truck type, load details, and pickup scheduled at least 48 hours ahead. Demonstrate that past dates are blocked.
5. Owner: Browse available jobs, filter by truck type/destination, submit a bid, update the same bid with a second amount.
6. Shipper: Accept the bid. Show the winner's phone and vehicle; contact sharing is restricted to participants.
7. Delivery: Send a private message, open a printable receipt, and play the clearly labeled offline truck simulation. The simulated animation does not affect real trip status.
8. Owner: Advance through pickup, in transit, and delivered. Shipper confirms completion and leaves a star rating + written review. Reopen the owner My Vehicle page to show the full customer feedback.
9. Admin: Display statistics, notifications, dispute report and admin resolution.
10. Security: Show Forgot Password request and the demo mailbox's one-time token. Briefly explain 15-minute expiry.
11. Responsiveness: Resize browser to ~375px and open /trucks to show its styled responsive profile.

Presentation note: Demo inbox is NOT an actual email provider, and tracking is NOT live GPS. Do not claim otherwise. Test on localhost before deploying to Render. No online payments are implemented.

Navigation: On refresh or after posting from a detail page, the browser preserves the previous scroll position; successful review posting opens the reviews section.
